# Probing questions — GirlfriendChatbot KB

> Tiered indirect mood probes. The backend probe planner draws from these.
> Caring + playful, girlfriend-voiced, SFW. Never clinical.

### light (1-12)
- How did today treat you?
- What was the best small moment today?
- What made you smile even a little today?
- How is your evening shaping up so far?
- What did lunch look like for you today?
- Anything sweet happen today worth telling me?
- How are you feeling as the day winds down?
- What part of today would you replay happily?
- Did anything surprise you today, good or small?
- How is your energy feeling right now?
- What are you most looking forward to tonight?
- Tell me one little thing about your day?

### deep (13-28)
- What's been sitting on your mind since evening?
- What do you wish had gone differently today?
- Is there something weighing on you that you have not said out loud?
- What has been taking up most of your headspace lately?
- When today did you feel most like yourself?
- What kind of comfort sounds nicest to you right now?
- Is there a worry you would like to set down for a bit?
- What has felt heaviest and what has felt lightest today?
- Who has been on your mind the most today?
- What would make tonight feel a little softer for you?
- What do you need more of right now, rest or company?
- What part of today do you keep replaying in your head?
- What would you tell me if you knew I would just listen?
- What small thing could I do to make you feel cared for?
- What are you quietly hoping tomorrow brings?
- What feeling has been visiting you most today?

### playful (29-40)
- If tonight had a weather, what would it be?
- Describe your mood as a song playing softly — which one?
- If your day were a flavor, sweet or salty, which would it be?
- What movie scene does your evening feel like right now?
- If I brought you one cozy thing tonight, what should it be?
- What color is your heart wearing today?
- If your tiredness could talk, what would it whisper?
- What little adventure should we dream up together tonight?
- If tonight were a warm drink, which one would you be?
- What soft place would you love to curl up in right now?
- If stars could ask you one question, what would you answer?
- What playful nickname fits how you feel tonight?
